# Fundamental Score: Detailed Scoring Criteria (0-10)

6 categories: 4 scored 0-2, 2 scored 0-1. Maximum 10 points.

## 1. Science & Platform Strength (0-2)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| **2** | Strongly differentiated science; validated or de-risked mechanism; platform generates multiple assets; external validation (KOLs, partners, published data) |
| **1** | Plausible, partially validated science; some differentiation; platform potential but unproven |
| **0** | Weak or non-differentiated mechanism; unclear biology; no platform leverage |

**Key questions:**
- Is the mechanism of action well-understood and validated?
- Does the company own a platform that can generate multiple clinical candidates?
- Have external experts or partners validated the science?
- Is there published peer-reviewed data supporting the approach?

## 2. Pipeline Depth & Breadth (0-2)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| **2** | Multiple credible shots on goal; diversified by indication, modality, or stage; clear sequencing strategy |
| **1** | Some pipeline depth but concentrated risk; limited diversification |
| **0** | Single-asset story; binary outcome risk |

**Key questions:**
- How many assets are in clinical or late preclinical development?
- Is there diversification across indications, targets, or modalities?
- Is there a logical sequencing strategy (lead asset de-risks followers)?
- What happens to the company if the lead asset fails?

## 3. Clinical Data Quality (0-2)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| **2** | Strong, consistent, statistically meaningful efficacy and safety; reproducible across cohorts or studies; well-designed trials |
| **1** | Early or mixed data; promising signals but not yet robust; small sample sizes |
| **0** | Weak, inconsistent, negative, or unsafe data; failed endpoints; irreproducible results |

**Key questions:**
- Were primary endpoints met with statistical significance?
- Is the safety profile clean and manageable?
- Are results consistent across patient subgroups and cohorts?
- How do results compare to standard of care and competitors?
- Were trials well-designed (randomized, controlled, adequate power)?

## 4. Management Quality & Execution (0-2)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| **2** | Proven operators with relevant biotech/pharma track record; strong capital allocation; transparent communication; consistent execution against stated timelines |
| **1** | Mixed track record; some execution gaps but generally competent; limited prior exits or approvals |
| **0** | Credibility issues; poor governance; repeated execution failures; excessive compensation relative to stage; opaque communication |

**Key questions:**
- Has this team taken drugs through FDA approval before?
- How does insider ownership and alignment look?
- Is capital allocation disciplined (not chasing too many targets)?
- Do they hit stated milestones on time?
- How transparent and consistent is investor communication?

## 5. Market Opportunity / Economic TAM (0-1)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| **1** | Realistic economic TAM >= $1B for the company's actual monetizable opportunity, adjusted for pricing, reimbursement, achievable market share, treatment duration, and competitive dynamics |
| **0** | Economic TAM < $1B or structurally constrained (pricing pressure, low penetration, short duration, dominant incumbents) |

**Critical distinction:** Economic TAM is NOT the total disease prevalence x list price. Adjust for:
- Realistic pricing and payer reimbursement
- Achievable market share given competition
- Treatment duration and persistence
- Geographic access and distribution
- Time to peak sales

## 6. Cash Runway & Balance Sheet (0-1)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| **1** | At least 2 years of cash runway at current or reasonably projected burn rate; low near-term dilution risk; clean balance sheet |
| **0** | < 2 years runway or high likelihood of near-term, punitive financing; significant debt overhang; history of dilutive raises at bad terms |

**Key questions:**
- Cash and equivalents vs quarterly burn rate = how many quarters?
- Are there upcoming milestones that could trigger non-dilutive funding (partnerships, grants)?
- What does the capital markets environment look like for this company?
- Any convertible notes, warrants, or other dilutive instruments coming due?
