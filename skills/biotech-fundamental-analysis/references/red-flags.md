# Competitive Landscape & Red Flag Analysis

## Competitive Positioning Assessment

For each company, evaluate and document:

| Dimension | What to Assess |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Direct competitors** | Who else targets the same indication with similar or different mechanisms? |
| **Mechanistic differentiation** | Is the MoA truly distinct, or a me-too with marginal improvement? |
| **Efficacy advantages** | Meaningful superiority in response rate, durability, survival, or biomarker endpoints? |
| **Safety advantages** | Cleaner side-effect profile vs competitors? Fewer black box warnings? |
| **Convenience advantages** | Oral vs IV, dosing frequency, route of administration, treatment duration? |
| **Economic advantages** | Lower cost of goods, pricing flexibility, favorable reimbursement positioning? |
| **Leader vs follower** | First-in-class, best-in-class, or fast-follower? Category-defining or catching up? |
| **Durability of advantage** | Is the moat structural (IP, platform, data) or temporary? |

## Red Flag Checklist

This layer validates the Fundamental Score. A company that scores well on the rubric can still be flagged and disqualified here. Run every category for every company.

### Scientific Red Flags

- [ ] Unvalidated or poorly understood mechanism of action
- [ ] Irreproducible preclinical data
- [ ] Reliance on a single KOL or research group for validation
- [ ] No peer-reviewed publications supporting the approach
- [ ] Mechanism previously failed in other companies' hands
- [ ] Platform technology with no demonstrated clinical translation
- [ ] Animal model relevance questionable for human disease

### Clinical Red Flags

- [ ] Failed primary endpoints in any trial
- [ ] Safety signals (liver toxicity, cardiac events, immunogenicity)
- [ ] Dose-limiting toxicities at therapeutic doses
- [ ] Inconsistent results across cohorts or studies
- [ ] Small, open-label, single-arm studies presented as definitive
- [ ] Cherry-picked subgroup analyses driving the narrative
- [ ] Trial design issues (underpowered, wrong comparator, soft endpoints)
- [ ] FDA Complete Response Letter (CRL) history
- [ ] Clinical hold history

### Commercial Red Flags

- [ ] Economic TAM overstated (headline vs realistic)
- [ ] Weak adoption dynamics (physician switching costs, formulary barriers)
- [ ] Dominant incumbent with entrenched market share
- [ ] Biosimilar or generic competition imminent
- [ ] Pricing pressure from payers or government action
- [ ] Limited distribution capability or commercial infrastructure
- [ ] No clear path to reimbursement

### Financial Red Flags

- [ ] < 2 years cash runway
- [ ] History of punitive, highly dilutive financings
- [ ] Excessive burn rate relative to pipeline stage
- [ ] Convertible notes or warrants creating dilution overhang
- [ ] Revenue miss or guidance cut (for commercial-stage)
- [ ] Dependence on a single partner for funding
- [ ] At-the-market (ATM) offering actively being used

### Management Red Flags

- [ ] No prior drug approvals on the team
- [ ] Executive turnover at key positions (CEO, CMO, CSO)
- [ ] Insider selling at scale without disclosed plan
- [ ] Opaque or inconsistent investor communication
- [ ] History of missed timelines or moved goalposts
- [ ] Excessive executive compensation relative to stage and cash
- [ ] Related-party transactions or governance concerns
- [ ] Board lacks relevant biotech/pharma expertise

## Using Red Flags

**Severity levels:**
- **Critical** (any one disqualifies): Failed pivotal trial, FDA clinical hold, < 6 months cash, fraud/governance scandal
- **Major** (2+ trigger downgrade): Safety signals, missed endpoints, executive exodus, punitive financing
- **Minor** (note but monitor): Single KOL reliance, small sample sizes, modest dilution, minor timeline slips

**Integration with Fundamental Score:**
- Critical red flag present → override score, flag as DO NOT BUY regardless of total
- 2+ Major red flags → reduce Fundamental Score by 1-2 points
- Minor red flags → note in report, no score adjustment unless pattern emerges
